# Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 Knowledge Base

## 1. Filing an RTI Application & Timelines
- **Statute**: Right to Information Act, 2005 (Section 6(1) & Section 7(1))
- **Jurisdiction**: All Central, State, and local public authorities, municipal corporations, public sector undertakings, and government-aided bodies.
- **Application Fee**: ₹10 (via Indian Postal Order, Court Fee stamp, or online payment at rtionline.gov.in). Free for Below Poverty Line (BPL) citizens.
- **Timelines**:
  - **Standard**: 30 calendar days from receipt.
  - **Life & Liberty**: 48 hours if information concerns individual liberty/life.
  - **Department Transfer**: 5 days if wrongly addressed (Section 6(3)).
- **No Justification Required**: The applicant is not required to explain why information is being sought.

---

## 2. RTI Appeals & PIO Penalties
- **First Appeal (Section 19(1))**: Within 30 days of 30-day timeline expiry or adverse reply, before the First Appellate Authority (FAA).
- **Second Appeal (Section 19(3))**: Within 90 days before Central Information Commission (CIC) or State Information Commission (SIC).
- **Penalties (Section 20)**: ₹250 per day (up to ₹25,000) on errant Public Information Officers.

---

## 3. Civic & Public Accountability Applications
- Road construction/repair tenders, contractor names, defect liability period.
- Municipal budget expenditure, sanction letters, building approvals.
- Government recruitment exam answer keys, marks lists, and cutoffs.
- Delay in public services (passport, police verification, pension, ration cards).
