# Indian Workplace Rights Knowledge Base

## 1. Unpaid Wages, Full & Final Settlement (FnF) & Relieving Letter
- **Statute**: Payment of Wages Act, 1936 & State Shops and Commercial Establishments Acts
- **Summary**: Employers cannot withhold earned wages, notice pay, or statutory letters (experience/relieving letter) upon resignation or termination.
- **Settlement Timeline**: Standard FnF clearance within 7 to 30 days of last working date.
- **Remedies**: Written demand notice -> Regional Labour Commissioner / Conciliation Officer -> Section 33C(2) Industrial Disputes Act recovery petition.

---

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH Act, 2013)
- **Statute**: POSH Act, 2013 (Section 4, 9, 11, 13)
- **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)**: Mandatory for all organizations with 10+ employees.
- **Complaint Window**: 3 months from the incident (extendable to 6 months).
- **Interim Reliefs**: Complainant can demand transfer, up to 3 months paid leave, and reporting restructuring.
- **Investigation Limit**: 90 days inquiry timeline.

---

## 3. Maternity Benefits & Non-Discrimination
- **Statute**: Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (Amended 2017)
- **Summary**: 26 weeks paid maternity leave. Discharge or dismissal during pregnancy or maternity absence is strictly unlawful under Section 12.

---

## 4. Wrongful Termination & Retrenchment Compensation
- **Statute**: Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (Section 25F) & State Shops & Establishments Acts
- **Summary**: Mandatory 30-day notice (or pay in lieu) plus 15 days average pay per completed year of service. Arbitrary terminations without due process violate natural justice.
