# Indian Tenant Rights Knowledge Base

## 1. Security Deposit Limits & Mandatory Refund
- **Statute**: Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Section 10 & 13) and State Rent Control Acts
- **Summary**: Residential security deposits cannot exceed 2 months' rent. The landlord must refund the deposit upon the tenant handing over vacant possession.
- **Permissible Deductions**: Only actual unpaid rent or documented damages beyond normal wear and tear.
- **Remedy**: 15-day formal demand notice, followed by filing before the local Rent Authority / Rent Tribunal with interest.

---

## 2. Protection Against Unlawful Eviction & Utility Disconnection
- **Statute**: Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Section 20 & 21), Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 106)
- **Summary**: Landlords are strictly prohibited from forcefully evicting tenants, changing locks, or disconnecting water, electricity, or gas supplies without a Rent Court order.
- **Notice Period**: Mandatory 30-day notice or as stipulated in a registered rent agreement.
- **Remedy**: Injunction petition before Rent Authority; police complaint for criminal trespass and intimidation.

---

## 3. Habitability & Essential Maintenance
- **Statute**: Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Section 15 & Schedule II)
- **Summary**: Landlord must maintain structural integrity, major plumbing, and exterior maintenance. Tenant may execute urgent repairs after 15 days' notice and deduct cost up to 50% of monthly rent.
