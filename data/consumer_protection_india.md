# Indian Consumer Protection Knowledge Base

## 1. Defective Goods, Deficient Services & Refund Rights
- **Statute**: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Section 2(11), Section 2(47), Section 35)
- **Product Liability**: Section 82 to 87 holds manufacturers and sellers liable for defective products.
- **Remedies**:
  - Right to repair, replacement, or 100% refund with interest.
  - Right to compensation for mental harassment and litigation expenses.
  - Filing portals: National Consumer Helpline (1915 / consumerhelpline.gov.in) and E-Daakhil (edaakhil.nic.in).

---

## 2. E-Commerce Protection & Dark Pattern Prohibition
- **Statute**: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 & CCPA Guidelines 2023
- **Grievance Redressal**: Mandatory Grievance Officer who must acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve within 1 month.
- **Return & Cancellation**: Unilateral cancellation penalties on consumers without reciprocal seller penalties are prohibited.
- **Dark Patterns**: Prohibits false urgency, drip pricing, basket sneaking, and disguised advertising.

---

## 3. Banking & Insurance Grievance Redressal
- **Statute**: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 & RBI / IRDAI Guidelines
- **Insurance Moratorium**: Claims cannot be rejected after 5-8 years of active policy for pre-existing diseases.
- **Zero Liability**: Fraudulent banking/UPI transactions reported within 3 working days carry zero liability for the customer.
